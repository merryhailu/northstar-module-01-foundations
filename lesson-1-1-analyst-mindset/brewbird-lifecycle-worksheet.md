## 1. Define

The question:
why has customer count decreased at downtown seattle cafe over the past 6 month, and how large is decline compared to other locations?

The decision this will inform:
Help the CEO decide whether the location needs changes such as renovation, management improvements, new promotions, or another solution.

What "done" looks like:
Identify the size of the customer decline, compare it with other stores, and provide evidence-based findings that can support the CEO's decision.

Customer transaction data for all 32 cafés
Sales revenue by location
Customer count by day or month
Product inventory data
Store location information
Employee scheduling or management data
Marketing and promotion records
Customer reviews or satisfaction ratings
Local competitor information (if available)

Datasets that already exist:
Sales transactions
Revenue reports
Inventory records
Employee management data
Promotion history
Customer loyalty or transaction data

Datasets that don't exist or aren't accessible:

Neighborhood events or construction near the Seattle store
Local population or foot traffic data

## 3. Clean

list 3 cleaning challenges:
duplicate customer or transaction records
missing promotion information
standardize date formats and product names

## 4. Analyze

List at least seven specific analytical questions:
How much has customer count changed over the last six months?
Is the Seattle location performing differently from the other 31 cafés?
Which products have increased or decreased in sales?
Did customer count change before or after any promotions?
correlation - Are customer review scores related to the decline in customer visits?
Are repeat customers decreasing more than first-time customers?
Are there seasonal trends that could explain the decline?

Name at least three visualization types :
Line chart: Show customer count trends over the last six months for the Seattle location.
Bar chart: Compare customer counts or sales across all café locations.

"The decline in customer count at the downtown Seattle café may be related to changes in customer behavior, store performance, or external factors. This analysis will investigate possible causes and provide recommendations based on the data."
